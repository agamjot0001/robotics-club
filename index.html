SHEET 01 / ABOUT

About the Club

A newly established club at GNDEC, built by students who wanted a place to build things with their hands — not just study how they work.

The Robotics Club started with a simple observation: engineering students spend years learning theory but rarely get a dedicated space to prototype, break things, and rebuild them. This club is that space — open to anyone at GNDEC who wants to work with motors, microcontrollers, sensors, and code.

Whether you're from Mechanical, Electronics, Computer Science, or any other branch, there's a bench here for you. We learn in public, fix things as a team, and treat every failed build as data for the next one.

Arduino & ESP32 Sensors & Actuators 3D Printing & CAD PCB Design Automation Basics Competition Bots

Vision

A campus where every engineering student has the tools, mentorship, and workshop time to turn a rough idea into a working machine.

Mission

  • Teach robotics and embedded systems through hands-on builds, not lectures alone.
  • Field student-led teams to represent GNDEC at competitions.
  • Bring mechanical, electronics, and software students onto the same projects.

SHEET 05 / JOIN

Join the Robotics Club

No prior robotics experience needed — just curiosity and a willingness to get your hands dirty.

Why join?

  • Work with real hardware, not just diagrams and theory.
  • Learn directly from teammates who are already building.
  • Get the chance to represent GNDEC at competitions.
  • Be part of the club's first-ever member cohort — help set how things are done.
